Edmonton-Specific Solar Incentives

Here's what's actually available for Edmonton homeowners right now, not a generic province-wide list.

Open

Edmonton CEIP (6.00%, 20-Year)

Edmonton's Clean Energy Improvement Program is open now for both residential and commercial applicants. The current 2026 rate is 6%, and residential applicants need a minimum of 3 upgrades bundled into the project.

Edmonton also runs a dedicated MURB solar rebate for multi-unit residential buildings (4+ dwellings): $0.50/watt up to $4,000 per dwelling, with a $100,000 annual program cap.

Learn how CEIP financing works or get your EPCOR site plans submitted early, typical review is 14 days.

🏡 The Solar Club™

Available to Edmonton homeowners on Rate Alberta billing, the Solar Club lets you switch between two seasonal electricity rates: a higher $0.30/kWh buy-back rate in the summer months when your system produces a surplus, and a lower $0.0877/kWh usage rate in winter.

Timing your rate switch to match your production can accelerate your break-even by 3-4 years compared to a flat rate. See how the Solar Club works.
