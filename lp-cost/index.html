What actually changes your break-even timeline

Net billing export credits

Every kWh your system exports earns a MicroGen credit against your bill. The more you export relative to what you use, the faster the system pays for itself.

Solar Club™ seasonal rate

Switching to the Solar Club™'s $0.30/kWh summer export rate can accelerate payback by 3 to 4 years for homes that export 70%+ of their usage.

CEIP financing, if your city qualifies

CEIP doesn't lower the total cost, it changes how you pay: $0 down, repaid through property taxes. Only available in participating cities with an open application window.
