Calgary CEIP (5.70%, 20-Year)

Calgary's Clean Energy Improvement Program residential intake is currently closed at capacity. Residential intake reopens September 22, 2026. Commercial CEIP launched March 2026 and is open now for business owners.

Calgary has the highest CEIP rate of any major Alberta municipality (5.70%) due to the highest demand in the province, but it's still well below conventional financing rates. When open, there's up to a 10% incentive on CEIP project costs.

Learn how CEIP financing works or get on the list now so your paperwork is ready when residential intake reopens.

🏡 The Solar Club™

Available to Calgary homeowners on Rate Alberta billing, the Solar Club lets you switch between two seasonal electricity rates: a higher $0.30/kWh buy-back rate in the summer months when your system produces a surplus, and a lower $0.0877/kWh usage rate in winter.

Timing your rate switch to match your production can accelerate your break-even by 3-4 years compared to a flat rate. See how the Solar Club works.
