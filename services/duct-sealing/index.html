Aeroseal Duct Sealing for Alberta Homes

If one room in your home is always cold, the basement feels drafty, or your furnace seems to run forever in January, there's a good chance you're losing conditioned air through leaky ductwork. Aeroseal seals those leaks from the inside, so the air you pay to heat and cool actually reaches your rooms.

What Is Aeroseal?

Aeroseal is an advanced duct sealing process that targets the small gaps and leaks hidden inside your duct system. In a lot of Alberta homes, especially ones with ducts running through unfinished basements, attics, or soffits, air leakage is more common than people realize.

Aeroseal seals those leaks by circulating a safe, non-toxic sealant through your ductwork. The sealant finds the leaks and seals them precisely from the inside out, without tearing into drywall or guessing where the problem is.

What Leaky Ducts Cost You

  • Higher utility bills, because you're paying to heat or cool air that never reaches your rooms
  • Hot and cold spots around the house
  • More dust and poor airflow
  • Extra wear on your furnace, AC, or heat pump
  • Longer run times and noisier operation

Why Duct Sealing Matters in Alberta

Alberta winters are no joke, and when it's minus 20 Celsius and the wind is ripping, duct leakage can really show up in your comfort and your bills. Even in newer builds, duct joints can shift, connections can loosen, and small gaps can add up over time. Aeroseal helps your system deliver the air where it's supposed to go, instead of dumping it into an attic or crawlspace you're not living in.

Benefits of Duct Sealing

1

Better energy efficiency

When your ducts are tight, less conditioned air is wasted. Your HVAC system doesn't have to work as hard to maintain temperature.

2

More even comfort

No more one bedroom freezing while the living room roasts. Sealed ducts help reduce hot and cold spots throughout the home.

3

Stronger airflow where you need it

If certain vents barely blow, duct leaks can be part of the issue. Aeroseal improves delivery and balance across the home.

4

Lower strain on your equipment

A furnace or heat pump fighting to keep up wears out faster. Sealed ducts can help reduce run time and stress on the system.

5

A cleaner, healthier home

Leaky ducts can pull dust in from attics, basements, or wall cavities. Tight ducts help reduce that drift and improve indoor air quality.

Is Aeroseal Right for Your Home?

Aeroseal can be a great fit if you're dealing with:

  • Uneven temperatures between rooms
  • A furnace that runs constantly in winter
  • Weak airflow from certain vents
  • Dusty rooms even after cleaning
  • A home that just never feels balanced
