Two Programs Do Most of the Work

Most of the payback math comes down to two things. Here's what they actually do.

Net Billing

Your panels send extra power to the grid in summer. That becomes a credit — sometimes called a Microgen Credit — applied straight to your bill. One real example: a $146.32 bill dropped to $21.16, an 85% reduction.

The Solar Club™

Switch between a summer export rate and a winter import rate with 10 days' notice, no penalty. That rate arbitrage is what turns summer surplus into lower winter bills.

CEIP Financing

In select cities, pay for your system through your property taxes instead of upfront. Windows are limited and can close fast.

How the Solar Club Rate Switch Pays You

Common Questions

What is net billing?
Net billing credits the extra electricity your panels send to the grid, applied directly to your bill. It's the main reason solar pencils out in Alberta.
How does the Solar Club actually work?
You switch between a summer export rate and a winter import rate with 10 days' notice. The spread between the two rates is what accelerates your payback.
Is CEIP available everywhere?
No — it's select cities only, repaid through property taxes, and windows can close quickly. We'll tell you straight if it applies to your address.
Do I need $200+ monthly bills to qualify?
That's the general minimum where the math starts working in your favor. Upload your bill and we'll tell you honestly either way.
